# Governance

Who owns what, who can see what, and what happens when people change.

## Ownership

- The device and all data on it are owned by the owners association or owners committee,
  purchased from the service charge budget with committee approval.
- The software is licensed to the building, not to the manager.
- The vendor never holds building data. The vendor holds signed model and software
  updates and the regulation corpus.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|------|-----|
| Committee | Everything. Set autonomy tiers, grant and revoke roles, read the full ledger, export |
| Manager | Operate day to day, confirm tier 3 actions, read everything except committee-only notes |
| Staff | See and complete assigned tasks, upload evidence |
| Contractor | See their own tasks and documents, upload evidence, receive chases |
| Auditor | Read-only, time-boxed, full archive and ledger |
| Vendor | Push signed updates. No data access. Remote attestation only |

## Root and recovery

- Root credentials are split between two committee members and a licensed escrow agent.
- Recovery requires any two of the three.
- The manager never holds root.

## Open questions to settle before the pilot

- Legal form of ownership in each jurisdiction (Dubai jointly owned property regime,
  Saudi HOA regulations, Abu Dhabi, Qatar, Oman).
- Whether a regulator will accept the ledger as the compliance record of truth.
- Data protection obligations under the UAE and Saudi personal data laws for staff and
  resident data held on the device, including CCTV.
